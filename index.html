Wearable spine · injury prevention

A spine that tells you when yours is rounding.

ExoSpot rides along the lifter’s back. Six thin film pressure sensors between the lower segments feel the spine bend, an ESP32 compares it with the lifter’s own range, and a light turns green, yellow or red during the rep.

How the light decides

Green0–60%Back neutral. Steady light.
Yellow60–80%Starting to round. Slow pulse.
Red80%+ · 0.1 sStop the set. Fast blink and buzz.

Percent of the lifter’s own maximum flexion, set at calibration: stand straight (0%), then bend forward slowly once (100%). Drag the slider or play three reps to see the device respond.

02 · Mechanical design

Rigid segments, ball joints, a flexure that pushes back

The chain bends forward, sideways and in twist like a spine. Two flexure strips on the outside get stiffer as the back rounds and pull it back toward neutral. Nothing is motorised.
